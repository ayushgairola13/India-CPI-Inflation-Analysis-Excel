# India CPI Inflation Analysis
## Project Overview
This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation trends using Microsoft Excel and time-series analysis techniques.
The analysis focuses on rural and urban CPI trends, inflation rates, the impact of COVID-19 and major economic events, and the relationship between oil prices and CPI movements.

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Excel Formulas
- Time-Series Analysis, Statistical Analysis
- Pivot Tables, Data Visualization

## Analysis Performed

- Analyzed rural and urban CPI trends to identify inflation patterns.
- Calculated Month-over-Month (MoM) and Year-over-Year (YoY) inflation rates.
- Compared inflation trends across rural and urban areas.
- Evaluated the impact of COVID-19 and major economic events on inflation.
- Analyzed the relationship between oil prices and CPI movements.
- Created Excel-based charts and visualizations to present key findings.

## Key Areas of Analysis
### 1. CPI Trend Analysis
Analyzed CPI movements over time and compared inflation trends between rural and urban segments.

### 2. MoM & YoY Inflation
Calculated short-term and annual inflation changes to understand the direction and pace of price movements.

### 3. Impact of Economic Events
Examined changes in inflation trends during COVID-19 and other major economic events.

### 4. Oil Prices vs CPI
Compared oil price movements with CPI trends to identify potential economic correlations.

## Key Insights

- Identified major periods of increase and decline in India's inflation rate.
- Compared differences in inflation patterns between rural and urban areas.
- Observed changes in inflation trends during major economic events.
- Investigated the relationship between oil prices and CPI movements.
